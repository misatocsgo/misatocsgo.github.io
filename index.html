FPS Mode

misatoCSGO includes a custom FPS Mode designed to reduce unnecessary visual effects and server-side processing for smoother gameplay.

use: !fps

FPS Mode disables or reduces things such as ragdolls, blood, gibs and other unnecessary effects while applying additional server-side performance optimizations.

can improve FPS by roughly 80%~ depending on the player's hardware, settings and situation.

FPS Mode can be toggled on or off at any time.
